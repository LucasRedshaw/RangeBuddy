Range Buddy

Play golf on the range using a simple decision tree.

How it works

  • 1. Hit your shot
  • 2. Consider if your shot was acceptable in the context of your own abilities
  • 3. Tap GOOD or POOR
  • 4. Follow the path
  • 5. Finish each hole

Why it works

Research in motor learning shows that random practice (switching tasks frequently) leads to better long-term performance than blocked practice (repeating the same shot). This is known as the contextual interference effect. While blocked practice can feel easier in the moment, random practice improves retention, adaptability, and transfer to real situations—like playing on a course.
